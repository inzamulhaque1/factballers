Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how Statflux Uploader (the "App") handles information. The App is a private tool operated by the Statflux team to upload and schedule videos on the Statflux YouTube channel that we own.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information we access

  • When the account owner signs in, Google provides an OAuth access token that lets the App upload videos and set video details (title, description, tags, thumbnail, privacy status and publish time) on that account's YouTube channel.
  • The App reads basic channel information (channel name and ID) to confirm it is uploading to the right channel.

The App does not access, collect or store any information about viewers, subscribers or any other YouTube user.

How information is stored

OAuth tokens are stored only on the team's own computer, in a local configuration file. They are never uploaded to any server, sold, or shared with third parties.

How information is used

Tokens are used solely to upload and manage videos on our own channel. No data is used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Data retention and deletion

  • The only data the App keeps is the OAuth token for our own account. It is kept for as long as we use the App and is deleted when the local configuration file is removed.
  • The App does not keep copies of any YouTube API data. Channel name and ID are read only at upload time to confirm the destination and are not stored.
  • To have any data deleted, email us at the address below and we will delete it within 7 days.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account security settings at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Revoking access immediately stops the App from using the token.

Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
